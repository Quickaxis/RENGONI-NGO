### Glassmorphism requirement

The navbar must use:
- `background: rgba(...)`
- `backdrop-filter: blur(...)`
- subtle border

- high transparency
- readable text over the page

### Mobile

Use a compact glass navigation:
- Rengoni logo
- menu trigger
- CTA if space permits
- smooth mobile menu
- no oversized mobile header

# 23. DESIGN QA CHECKLIST

Before declaring the implementation complete, compare the rendered website against the supplied reference.

Check:

### Header
- Does the navigation density resemble the reference?
- Is the navbar transparent/glass?
- Is the CTA positioned correctly?

### Hero
- Is the left/right balance similar?
- Is the headline scale correct?
- Is photography the dominant visual?
- Is the composition organic rather than geometric?

### Cards
- Are card proportions similar?
- Is the image-to-text relationship similar?
- Are shadows subtle?
- Are corners controlled?

### Sections
- Is there enough whitespace?
- Does each section have a clear visual hierarchy?
- Does the page flow like an editorial story?

### Colors
- Is the page predominantly warm white?
- Are accents restrained?
- Is typography dark and highly readable?

### Overall
Ask:

**“Does this look like the same design language as the supplied reference, with Rengoni content substituted into it?”**

If the answer is no, do not continue adding sections. Fix the composition first.
